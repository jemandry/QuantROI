## Target Customer Segments

### Primary: Registered Investment Advisors (RIAs)
- **Pain Points**: Manual compliance, limited AI tools, complex reporting
- **Value Proposition**: Automated SEC compliance, causal market insights, ZKP privacy
- **Pricing**: $499-$2K/month based on AUM and features

### Secondary: Individual Investors (Retail)
- **Pain Points**: Complex financial decisions, lack of personalized guidance
- **Value Proposition**: Storytelling UX, voice-guided investing, causal explanations
- **Pricing**: Freemium to $9.99/month premium

### Tertiary: Hedge Funds & Institutional Investors
- **Pain Points**: Alpha generation, risk management, regulatory compliance
- **Value Proposition**: Advanced causal analysis, custom APIs, enterprise security
- **Pricing**: Custom enterprise deals ($10K+ setup, revenue sharing)

## Competitive Positioning

### Unique Differentiators
1. **Causal AI**: First platform to offer true causal market analysis
2. **ZKP Privacy**: Enterprise-grade privacy without sacrificing compliance
3. **Storytelling UX**: Magical user experience with voice interface
4. **Perpetual Innovation**: Continuous feature evolution based on user feedback

### Competitive Landscape
- **Traditional RIA Software**: Outdated, manual processes

## Success Measurement Framework

### Leading Indicators
- Website traffic and conversion rates
- Demo requests and trial signups
- Content engagement and social shares
- Partnership pipeline development

### Lagging Indicators
- Monthly recurring revenue (MRR) growth
- Customer acquisition cost (CAC) optimization

- Net promoter score (NPS) maintenance

### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- **Growth**: 20% month-over-month user growth
- **Retention**: 40% annual retention rate
- **Revenue**: $100K MRR by Month 6, $1M by Month 12
- **Efficiency**: 3:1 LTV:CAC ratio
- **Quality**: 90% NPS score maintenance
